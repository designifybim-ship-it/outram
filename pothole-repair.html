Our why

Why roads matter to us

Every day, Barbadians drive around potholes on their way to work, school and home. Behind every pothole is a driver, a family and a journey that should end safely.

At Outram, we believe road maintenance is more than filling holes. It is about safety, protecting livelihoods and improving everyday life. The black stroke in our logo stands for asphalt and the roads we're working towards repairing.
