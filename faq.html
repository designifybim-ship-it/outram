Renting

Do you deliver the mixer?

Yes, anywhere in Barbados. Our haulers drop off and collect. Delivery is $80 to $120 depending on your parish.

Do I need electricity?

No. The MT-480 runs on a 9 HP diesel engine, and every rental arrives with a full tank.

What if it breaks down?

Malfunctions are entirely on us. Stop using it and call us. You'll get a replacement, or an extension on your rental if you have to wait on repairs.

I've never used a mixer. Is that OK?

Not a problem. We give first-time users instructions at handover so you can run it safely. Read through the rental agreement, and call us during your rental if you need support.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our Republic Bank business account, or in cash at our base at Byde Mill, St. George. See how to pay.

Can I pick up or drop off the mixer myself?

No. For your safety, and because of the limits of our insurance policy, our haulers handle every delivery and pick-up. Tell us where and when, and we'll do the moving.

How much is delivery?

$80 to $120, depending on your parish. It's $80 to Christ Church, St. Philip, St. George and St. Michael, and parts of St. Thomas, St. James, St. Joseph and St. John. We confirm the exact price when you book.

Does it come with fuel?

Yes. Every mixer arrives fuelled and ready to start mixing.

How much does it cost to rent?

Rates depend on how long you need the mixer. Book online or message us with your dates for the current rate.

How far ahead should I book?

Book a few days ahead so your mixer is on site when you're ready. Planning a Saturday job? Message us by midweek.

Booking and buying

What kind of mixer do you rent?

A heavy-duty 2-bag concrete mixer, model MT-480, with a 9 HP diesel engine. It runs anywhere on site, so you don't need electricity.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp 246-822-3288, or book online. Ask about rates, fees and terms, then we confirm your dates, site location and payment, and schedule delivery.

Can I buy a mixer instead?

Yes. Buy one outright, or ask about rent-to-own and pay over time until the mixer is yours. Message us for current prices.

What do I need to do when the rental ends?

Return the mixer clean and undamaged, and settle any outstanding fees when it's collected. Tell us how it went. Your feedback helps us improve.

Repairs, hours and asphalt

Do you repair mixers?

Yes. We service and repair concrete mixers, including emergency call-outs when a mixer stops on the job.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. We're closed on Sundays and public holidays. Call or WhatsApp 246-822-3288.

When do asphalt services start?

Soon. We're adding pothole repairs, utility cut repairs, failed patch replacement and small-area restoration for driveways and car parks. Message us to join the waitlist.

Where are you based?

Byde Mill, St. George. We deliver island-wide from there.
