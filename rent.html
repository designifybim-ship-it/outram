Checklist

Before you pour

A checklist to run through before your mixer arrives.

  • Clear access

    Make sure our haulers can reach the spot where the mixer will sit.

  • Materials ready

    Cement, sand, stone and water on site before you start.

  • Helpers lined up

    Mixing goes faster with hands for loading, wheeling and finishing.

  • Book ahead

    Message us early so your mixer is there when you need it. Planning a Saturday job? Message us by midweek.

  • Plan your timing

    Start early and avoid the hottest part of the day.

  • Pour all day

    A full tank keeps you mixing, and we collect once the job is done.

Mixer care

Keep your mixer running

Simple habits that keep the drum clean, the engine happy and your job moving all day.

  • Rinse after every batch

    Don't let concrete harden inside the drum. A quick rinse saves hours of chipping.

  • Don't overload

    Stick to the 2-bag capacity. Overloading strains the engine and the gears.

  • Check the fuel

    Keep the diesel topped up so the mixer doesn't stop halfway through a batch.

  • Problem? Stop and call

    If something sounds wrong, stop using it and call us. We'll replace it or extend your rental.
