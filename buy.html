Work smarter

Still mixing by hand?

Mixing concrete with a shovel is slow, tiring and hard to keep consistent. A mixer changes that.

By hand

  • Hours of back-breaking work
  • Uneven mixes from batch to batch
  • More helpers needed
  • Small pours only

With a mixer

  • Two bags mixed per batch
  • Even, consistent concrete
  • Fewer hands, less strain
  • Bigger pours, done sooner

Let the shovel retire. It's earned it.
