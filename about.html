About Outram

A Barbadian business based at Byde Mill, St. George, renting, selling and servicing concrete mixers, and working towards safer roads.

Meet the founder

“I was working a 9 to 5 and wanted a 5 to 9.”

Ijariah Outram started the mixer business because he was looking for extra income. Over time it became about serving the community.

Seeing opportunities in Barbados' construction industry, particularly the need for accessible equipment and better infrastructure, motivated him to start Outram.

What drives him is turning an idea into something meaningful: building from the ground up, solving real problems and creating opportunities for others. It's about more than selling or renting equipment. It's about making equipment ownership more accessible, helping other entrepreneurs grow, and eventually helping improve Barbados' roads.

“

I didn't want to simply identify problems. I wanted to be part of the solution.

Ijariah OutramFounder of Outram
Our mission

What we're building

Outram Concrete & Asphalt Solutions Inc is a Barbados-based construction equipment company specialising in equipment sales, rentals, repairs and flexible rent-to-own opportunities. It provides practical, dependable equipment and support to contractors, builders and individuals, helping make construction more accessible and productive, and it is expanding into asphalt maintenance and infrastructure solutions.

Accessible equipment

Rent, buy or rent-to-own, so more people can get the tools to do the job.

Growing entrepreneurs

Helping other small businesses take on bigger work and grow.

Better roads

Expanding into asphalt repairs, because road maintenance is about safety and livelihoods.

Our logo

The meaning behind our logo

Every part of the Outram mark tells our story: where we started in concrete, where we're going with asphalt, and the community we're building with.

  1. 1
    Safety yellow

    The colour of every job site in Barbados. It stands for safety, energy and getting the work done.

  2. 2
    The black road

    A bold black stroke for asphalt and the roads Outram is working towards repairing.

  3. 3
    Building upward

    A peak pointing up, like a roof or a foundation rising. It stands for growth, for customers and for Outram.
