Know the signs

Signs your mixer needs us

Making noise

Grinding, knocking or squealing from the gears or engine.

Losing power

The engine bogs down or struggles under a normal load.

Not turning

The drum won't spin, or spins unevenly.

Build-up in the drum

Hardened concrete slowing the mix down.

Mixer care

Keep your mixer running

Simple habits that keep the drum clean, the engine happy and your job moving all day.

  • Rinse after every batch

    Don't let concrete harden inside the drum. A quick rinse saves hours of chipping.

  • Don't overload

    Stick to the 2-bag capacity. Overloading strains the engine and the gears.

  • Check the fuel

    Keep the diesel topped up so the mixer doesn't stop halfway through a batch.

  • Problem? Stop and call

    If something sounds wrong, stop using it and call us. We'll replace it or extend your rental.
