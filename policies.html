Rental policies

Our policies

Renting with Outram

Please read these before you book. Questions? Call or WhatsApp 246-822-3288.

  • Delivery and collection

    Our haulers handle every delivery and pick-up, for your safety and because of our insurance. Delivery costs $80 to $120, depending on your parish, and is scheduled once your booking is confirmed.

  • Booking and payment

    Ask about rates, fees and terms before you book. We confirm your dates, site location and how you'd like to pay.

  • Rental agreement

    Read and understand the rental agreement before signing. Get familiar with the mixer before you start, and use it safely.

  • If something goes wrong

    Malfunctions are entirely on us. Stop using the mixer straight away and call us. You'll get a replacement, or an extension on your rental if you have to wait on repairs.

  • Returns and final settlement

    Return the mixer clean and undamaged. Settle any outstanding fees when the mixer is collected. Tell us how it went. Your feedback helps us improve.

  • Hours and contact

    Monday to Saturday, 9am to 5pm. Closed Sundays. Call or WhatsApp 246-822-3288.
