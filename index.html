FAQs

Our frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what people ask before they book. More on the FAQ page.

Do you deliver the mixer?

Yes, anywhere in Barbados. Our haulers drop off and collect. Delivery is $80 to $120 depending on your parish.

Do I need electricity?

No. The MT-480 runs on a 9 HP diesel engine, and every rental arrives with a full tank.

What if it breaks down?

Malfunctions are entirely on us. Stop using it and call us. You'll get a replacement, or an extension on your rental if you have to wait on repairs.

I've never used a mixer. Is that OK?

Not a problem. We give first-time users instructions at handover so you can run it safely. Read through the rental agreement, and call us during your rental if you need support.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer to our Republic Bank business account, or in cash at our base at Byde Mill, St. George. See how to pay.
